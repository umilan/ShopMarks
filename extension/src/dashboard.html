Connect Dropbox

Sync goes through your own Dropbox app. Once per Dropbox account:

  1. In the Dropbox App Console, create an app: Scoped access, App folder.
  2. Permissions: tick files.content.read and files.content.write, then Submit.
  3. Settings → OAuth 2: allow public clients, and add this redirect URI:
  4. Copy the App key from Settings and paste it here. It is kept on this device only.

How to use ShopMarks

Save a product

  • Open a product page in any online shop and click the ShopMarks icon in the toolbar. You can also press Alt+Shift+S, or right-click the page and choose Save this product to ShopMarks.
  • The popup reads the title, picture, price and currency. Change anything before saving; step through the pictures with ‹ ›.
  • Pick one or more lists, or type a new list name at the bottom of the picker. The lists you used last time are chosen already.
  • The speech-bubble button next to the lists adds a note.
  • Price or name read wrong? Click the target button next to it, then click the right text on the page. ShopMarks remembers that spot for the shop.
  • Opening the popup on a page you already saved lets you update it, and a new price is added to its price history.

Your saved items

  • Open this page from the logo in the popup.
  • Lists are on the left (on small screens, in the dropdown at the top): All Items, Unsorted and your own lists. + creates a list; ⋯ on a list renames or removes it.
  • Search, sort and switch between grid and list view at the top.
  • Point at a card to edit it (title, price, lists, note, picture) or delete it. Tick its checkbox to select several, then use Select all or Delete selected.
  • Drag cards onto a list to move them there, or onto Trash to delete them.
  • + in the top bar adds an item from a link.
  • Update prices in the settings menu reads the current price of every item shown. Chrome asks for access to those shops first.

Keep a copy

  • Export saves all items and lists to a JSON file; Import adds them back from one.
  • Backup folder: pick a folder (one inside Dropbox works well) and ShopMarks rewrites saver-backup.json there after every change. Its ⋯ has Manage and Reset to default.

Sync and your phone

  • Sync with Dropbox keeps one file in your Dropbox that every connected device shares. The first time, it shows the setup steps and asks for your Dropbox App key.
  • This page also runs as a web app on a phone: open it in Safari, choose Share → Add to Home Screen, then connect Dropbox there too.
  • The web app cannot read shop pages: it saves the link, and the price fills in on the next Update prices on a computer.

Install the extension

  • Download Chrome extension (.zip) from the settings menu and unzip it.
  • Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose the extension folder inside.
